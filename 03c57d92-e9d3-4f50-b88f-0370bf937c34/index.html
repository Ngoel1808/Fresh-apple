About AppleFresh

Enjoy our farm's fresh harvest straight to your home.

At AppleFresh, we believe in bringing you the purest and freshest apples, directly hand-harvested from high-altitude orchards in Himachal and Kashmir.

Our mission is to provide high-quality, naturally grown apples while supporting local farming communities and promoting a healthy lifestyle.
